Privacy Policy

Viventia · Effective 5 August 2026 · Version 1.0

Viventia keeps what you enter on your device. There is no account, no server run by Viventia, and no database anyone else can read. Your food logs, weight history, workouts, habits and budget figures are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

1. The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • There is no cloud storage. Viventia has no server of its own.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics in this version.
  • Nothing is shared with third parties, because nothing leaves your device in the first place.
  • The only data that ever leaves your device is a backup file that you choose to export, to a location you choose.

2. What you can enter, and where it stays

Viventia is a general wellness and organisational tool. Depending on which parts you use, you may enter:

  • Health and body information — sex, age, height, weight, activity level and your goal, plus any weigh-ins you record over time.
  • Nutrition information — meals and foods you log, along with their calorie and macronutrient figures.
  • Fitness information — the training programme you picked and the sessions you mark as completed.
  • Habits — which habits you switched on and which days you ticked them.
  • Budgeting information — income, expenses, categories, savings goals, and any assets or debts you list.
  • Preferences — language, currency, time zone and theme.

All of it is written to your device's local storage and stays there. On Android it lives inside the app's private storage; on the web it lives in your browser's storage for this site. It is not transmitted to the developer, and the developer has no ability to read it, recover it or delete it for you.

Some of this is sensitive — information about your body, your eating and your money. That is precisely why Viventia is built without a server: there is no copy of it anywhere for anyone to lose, sell or be compelled to hand over.

3. Backups you export yourself

Because there is no cloud backup, Viventia can write your data to a file so you do not lose it. This only ever happens when you tap Export. It is never automatic and never scheduled.

  • On Android, the system's own file picker opens and you choose where the file is saved. If you cancel, nothing is written.
  • On the web, the file downloads through your browser in the ordinary way.
  • The file is plain, readable JSON. It contains everything you have entered, including health and financial figures.
  • Once saved, that file is yours and is outside Viventia's control. If you put it in a cloud drive, send it to yourself, or share the device, that copy is governed by whatever service or person now holds it. Please treat it as you would any personal record.

Restoring works the same way in reverse: you pick a backup file, and it replaces what is currently on the device.

4. What Viventia does not do

In this version, Viventia does not:

  • create accounts, or ask for an email address, name or password;
  • upload, sync or store your data on any server;
  • show advertisements or use an advertising identifier;
  • include analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or any tracking software;
  • use cookies for tracking or advertising;
  • sell, rent, share or disclose your information to anyone;
  • build a profile of you or make automated decisions about you;
  • request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, health platform data or any connected device or sensor.

About the network permission

The Android app declares the standard internet permission. This is a technical requirement: the app's own screens are served to it from inside the app package, and Android will not load them without that permission.

Being straightforward about it: that permission does grant real network capability, and you are right to expect an explanation rather than a reassurance. The app makes no requests to any external server, and it runs entirely in aeroplane mode. If that ever changes, this policy will change with it, beforehand.

5. Deleting your data

You are in complete control, and you never need to ask the developer — which is fortunate, because the developer has nothing to delete.

  • Inside the app. Open More → Start again and choose to erase everything. This clears every log, weigh-in, expense and goal from the device.
  • Clear the app's storage. On Android, open your device's Settings → Apps → Viventia → Storage and clear it. In a browser, clear the site data for this site.
  • Uninstall. Removing the app removes the data with it.

All three are permanent and cannot be undone. There is no server-side copy to restore from. If you might want your data later, export a backup first.

6. Security, described honestly

Your data is protected primarily by your device: its lock screen, its encryption and its separation between apps. Android keeps each app's storage private to that app.

Two things you should know rather than assume:

  • Viventia does not encrypt your data itself. It relies on the protection your device already provides.
  • The optional PIN on the Money screen hides that screen from someone glancing at your phone. It is not encryption, and it does not stop anyone with deeper access to the device from reading the underlying data.

If your device is lost, shared or compromised, the data in Viventia is as exposed as anything else on it.

7. Children

Viventia is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. It does not calculate calorie targets for anyone who enters an age under 18.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. In Viventia these are exercised directly and immediately, by you:

  • Access and portability — export a backup; it is a complete, readable copy of everything stored.
  • Rectification — edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Erasure — see section 5.

The developer cannot action these requests on your behalf, because the developer never receives your data and cannot identify you. If you have a concern about this policy, please write to info.yobe.creative@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority; in the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

9. Changes to this policy

If Viventia ever gains a feature that sends data off your device — a sync service, analytics or advertising — this policy will be updated and the effective date changed before that feature ships, and the app will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
